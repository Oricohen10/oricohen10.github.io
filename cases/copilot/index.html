Agent Copilot is a real-time AI panel embedded in Verint's contact center desktop. It reads the conversation as it happens and surfaces what the agent needs - before they have to search.

Background

Verint had best-in-class tools for routing, recording, and workforce management - but nothing for the agent in the middle of a live conversation. Agent Experience was the initiative to change that, and the Copilot panel was the bet at its center.

It started as a hackathon. The concept - a panel that reads what's happening in real time and responds with context, knowledge, and guidance - didn't exist anywhere in Verint's product suite. It was a blank page problem, which meant it was also a real design problem.

The problem

Agents are expected to know everything - instantly. While a customer is talking, they hold context, search knowledge bases, stay on top of compliance requirements, manage the conversation itself, and write a wrap-up when it's done.

All of it is manual. All of it adds up. Nobody was helping the agent.

The Copilot

A dedicated panel on the right side of the agent desktop. It tracks the conversation and responds - no search, no switching screens, no extra effort.

01 Before the agent says hello

Context from the start

When a customer escalates from the bot to a live agent, the Copilot has already read the entire prior conversation. A natural-language summary waits in the acceptance screen - who they are, what they want, what they already tried. The agent picks up exactly where the bot left off.

02 Answers before you ask for them

Real-time knowledge

The Copilot reads the conversation and surfaces the right knowledge card automatically. When the topic shifts to credit scores, the rates table appears. When a compliance requirement is triggered, a coaching nudge fires. Agents can also ask directly and get a structured answer - with push-to-chat and send-via-email built into every response.

03 Wrap-up that writes itself

Call complete summary

When the customer leaves, the Copilot generates the full wrap-up - outcome, key points, next steps, notes. The agent reviews and edits before saving. Two to three minutes of manual write-up becomes a 20-second review.

Result

The Copilot was mature enough to ship standalone - ahead of the full Agent Experience launch. It became the first AI-first feature in Verint's contact center desktop, and the first time this category of real-time assistance was shown at Verint Engage.
