Agent Copilot is a real-time AI panel embedded in Verint's contact center desktop. It reads the conversation as it happens and surfaces what the agent needs - before they have to ask. First of its kind at Verint, designed and shipped in 2024.

The problem

Contact center agents are expected to know everything - instantly. While a customer is talking, they hold context, search knowledge bases, remember product rules, handle the conversation, and write up a wrap-up when it ends. All of that is manual, all of it adds up, and none of it is where agents want to spend their attention.

Existing agent desktops gave agents better tools for managing conversations. No product at Verint gave them real-time help during them. The Copilot panel was the answer to that gap.

The Copilot panel

A dedicated right-hand panel in the agent desktop that tracks the conversation in real time and responds - surfacing context, knowledge, coaching, and summaries without the agent leaving their screen.

01 Before the agent says hello

Self-Service Summary

When a customer is escalated from the bot to a live agent, the Copilot has already read the entire prior conversation. A natural-language summary waits in the acceptance modal - who the customer is, what they want, what they already tried. Zero ramp-up time.

02 Who you're talking to

Customer Insights

Account data, channel history, contact reason, and customer tags - rendered as a scannable fact sheet. Agents know "existing customer, Premier tier, called 2 days ago, calling about mortgages" in the first two seconds.

03 Answers that appear before you ask

Proactive Knowledge

The Copilot reads the conversation and surfaces the relevant knowledge card automatically. When the customer mentions buying a home, the mortgage pre-approval step appears. When they ask about credit scores, the rates table is already there. Each card has push-to-chat and send-via-email actions built in.

04 Catches what's easy to miss

Real-time Coaching

Nudge cards surface mid-conversation when the Copilot detects a missed opportunity or compliance requirement. Agents under pressure forget things - the Copilot doesn't.

When the customer leaves, the Copilot generates a full call summary - outcome, key points, next steps, additional notes. Agents review and edit before saving. What used to be two to three minutes of manual wrap-up becomes a 20-second edit.

How it got built

This started as a hackathon. The concept - a real-time AI panel that reads the conversation and responds in kind - didn't exist anywhere in Verint's product suite. The hackathon gave us permission to try it fast.

The challenge was scope: the panel had to handle five distinct interaction patterns (summary, context, knowledge, coaching, wrap-up) while feeling like a single coherent assistant, not five separate widgets. Every iteration had to answer the same question - does this help the agent, or does it just add noise?

Origin
Hackathon. First prototype of the real-time suggestion panel - no existing product to compare against, no internal template to follow. We were defining a new category.
Design
Dozens of iterations in Figma. The constraints were hard: the panel could not distract from the conversation. Cards had to appear at the right moment and recede when not needed. Timing and hierarchy were everything.
Demo
Presented at Verint Engage 2024 as a Figma prototype. Full end-to-end scenario: customer on mobile, bot handoff, live agent conversation, AI coaching, call wrap-up. First time this category of AI assistance had been shown at Verint.
Shipped
The Copilot was mature enough to ship standalone, ahead of the full Agent Experience launch. It became the first AI-first feature in Verint's contact center desktop.
Result

The Copilot shipped as a standalone feature ahead of the wider Agent Experience launch. Early business cases validated the core premise: when agents have in-the-moment access to the right knowledge, calls get shorter, outcomes improve, and agents spend less time on overhead.
